Statistical characterization of restaurant customer variables into Qualitative and Quantitative scales.

Statistical Concept Reference Fundamental Concepts

Qualitative vs Quantitative

Qualitative (Categorical): Non-numeric characteristics labeled by attributes (e.g. Day, Smoker). Cannot perform direct arithmetic operations.

Quantitative (Numerical): Measurable quantities where mathematical calculations (Mean, Standard Deviation) are valid.

Discrete vs Continuous

Discrete (size): Result of counting. Values proceed in isolated integer steps without intermediates.

Continuous (total_bill, tip): Result of measurement along an uninterrupted real continuum.

Scales of Measurement

Nominal: Mutual exclusivity without ranking (Sex, Day).

Ratio: True zero point exists; meaningful ratios (e.g. a ₹40 bill is twice as large as a ₹20 bill).
